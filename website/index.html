Understanding PCOS & How Sakhi Helps

How PCOS Impacts Your Health 🩺

  • Hormonal Imbalance: Elevates levels of male hormones (androgens), causing irregular menstrual cycles, persistent acne, and unwanted facial/body hair growth.
  • Metabolic Shifts: Can lead to insulin resistance, making it easier to gain weight and significantly harder to shed weight without tailored PCOS-specific nutrition.
  • Energy & Fatigue: Chronic fatigue and sleep apnea are common, directly impacting daily productivity and cognitive focus.
  • Mental Well-being: Increases risks of anxiety, depression, and body image concerns due to structural hormonal fluctuations.
